## Overview

Tailoring a CV by hand meant slow-and-good or fast-and-generic. This Claude skill drafts a CV in my template from a maintained knowledge base, anchoring every version to source documents so "tailored" never drifts into "embellished."

## How it works

- **Structured knowledge base** of my experience, kept separate from any single CV.
- **Template-aware generation** that fills my existing two-column layout.
- **Per-application tailoring** that picks relevant experience without inventing anything.
- **Real `.docx` output**, ready to send.
