A small vibe-coded game for children learning to put words together. You type a word (or roll a random one), then spell it out by clicking tiles on a colourful Czech alphabet — the right letter jumps into its slot, the wrong one just wiggles. Finish a word and confetti arrives.

It covers the full Czech alphabet including diacritics, gives the CH digraph its own tile without forcing it, and has a hidden-word mode for spelling from memory rather than copying. Three static files, no build, no dependencies.
