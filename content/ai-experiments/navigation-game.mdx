A small canvas game about patience and aim. Balloons drift slowly across the sky, each one carrying a car on a timber deck. Prick the envelope with the pin and the balloon sinks gently to the road, where the car drives itself into your garage. Let one drift off-screen and it's gone — the garage keeps count of the ones that got away.

There are 18 car models across four rarities, from the Bean to the Aurora. Rarity drives both how often a model shows up and the colour of the balloon carrying it, so a gold envelope is worth chasing. Each car is drawn from a short spec, so adding a model is a few lines. The collection persists in local storage. Plain canvas and JavaScript, no build step.
