A direct-owner listing for a house sale — the kind of thing a real estate agent would normally build and take a cut for. Built it myself instead: a video walkthrough, room-by-room floor plans across three usable layouts (multi-generational home, three separate units, or living space plus a office/practice), a photo gallery, and a straightforward, fair asking price up front instead of the usual negotiation theatre.

It's a side business, not a case study in the usual sense here — no roadmap, no personas, just a real sale that needed a page good enough to make the case for the house on its own.
