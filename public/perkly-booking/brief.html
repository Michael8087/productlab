PERKLY · BOOKING AT PURCHASE

Turn a purchase into a booked visit

A speculative product exercise — Perkly is a fictional deals marketplace.

The problem

Every day people buy a massage, a facial or a haircut on Perkly and then have to phone the salon to get a slot. Most merchants run on a booking tool that does not talk to the marketplace, or on a paper diary.

Why now

Connecting to merchants used to mean one booking-engine integration at a time, months each. The cost of trying things has collapsed:

  • → A simple booking engine for small businesses can be built in days, not quarters.
  • → Showing availability before the purchase is technically within reach.
  • → An AI assistant that books on the customer's behalf, over phone, email or text, is an option on the table.

The goal

One number: the share of Health & Beauty purchases that end in a confirmed booking.

Several roads, one budget: decide which road gets it and which one is dropped. Merchant adoption is part of the job, because a product nobody switches on is a demo.

What this prototype shows

  • → Picking a time while buying, with availability coming from the merchant's side.
  • → Three ways to reach the merchant: a calendar (live Google Calendar), the Perkly Merchant app for merchants without a dedicated booking tool, and a booking-platform API (live Square sandbox).
  • → Buying now and booking later, with a reminder that brings the customer back.
  • → Requests the merchant confirms in one tap, or answers with another time.

Use it

Press Run demo at the top for a guided run, or click around the phone in the middle and switch the merchant side on the right.

All deals, merchants, prices and bookings are synthetic demo data.
