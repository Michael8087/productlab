Privacy

This is a personal demo prototype, not a Perkly product and not a commercial service.

What "Sign in with Google" does

If you choose to sign in, the page asks Google for permission to read and create events in your Google Calendar (scope calendar.events). It uses this only to show which times are busy and to add a booking event when you book a demo deal.

What is stored

  • Access token: kept in your browser's memory only. It is gone when you close or reload the page.
  • Calendar data: read directly from Google into your browser. It is not sent to any server of mine and not saved anywhere.
  • Events written: bookings you make are created in your own Google Calendar. You can delete them there at any time.
  • Browser storage: demo state (purchases, demo bookings) stays in your browser's local storage on this device.

No analytics, no sharing

The site has no backend, no analytics and no ads. Nothing about you is collected, sold or shared.

Revoking access

Use "Sign out" in the calendar panel, or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Unverified app notice

Google may show "Google hasn't verified this app". That is expected for a personal demo. Continue only if you are comfortable with the description above.
